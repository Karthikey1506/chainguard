Why Graph Database?

Recursive Depth: Electronics bills of materials have recursive parent-child relationships of varying depth. Graph databases support variable-length path queries (e.g. [:USED_IN*1..5]) without requiring fixed-depth relational joins.

Traceability: In a single query, we can trace component links all the way from raw silicone or fabrication plants up to assembly lines and final consumer goods.

Resilience: When nodes are disrupted, graph traversals trace the affected downstream path directly to identify blocked SKUs and calculate cumulative monthly revenue risk.
